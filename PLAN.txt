Shadows

[Shadow Distance]

Description: Adjusts the rendering distance at which shadows begin to appear.
Values: Minimum Distance = 0.16 (default distance of 0.16) up to 0.64 (or higher if needed).
Type: Slider

[Shadow Quality]

Description: Adjusts the quality of shadows.
Values: 512, 1024, 2048, 3072, 4096
Type: Slider

[Shadow Blur]

Description: Adjusts how shadows appear; low values make shadows very sharp
Values: OFF, Low, Medium, High
Type: Toggle

Lighting

[Hand-Held Lighting]

Description: Enables or disables lighting when picking up or holding torches and lanterns
Values: True or False
Type: Toggle

Effects

[Anti-Aliasing]

Description: Anti-aliasing that improves image quality
Values: OFF, TAA, FXAA
Type: Toggle

[Leaf Animation]

Description: Adjusts the animation speed of tree leaves
Values: 0.00 - 1.00 (0.05 Default)
Type: Slider

[Wave Animation]

Description: Adjusts the speed of the water wave animation
Values: 0.00 - 1.00 (0.05 Default)
Type: Slider

Water 

[Water Type]

Description: Changes the appearance of the water
Values: Enhanced, Realistic
Type: Toggle

Enhanced = slightly simpler water, still focused on performance (you can enable reflections and effects; option to use the game’s vanilla water texture)
Realistic = realistic water with adjustable reflections and effects.
